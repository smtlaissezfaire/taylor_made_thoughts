Episode 9

How Will You Measure Your Life?: Finding fulfilment using lessons from some of the world's greatest businesses (by Clayton Christensen, James Allworth, and Karen Dillon)

July 17, 2026

In this episode, I discuss How Will You Measure Your Life? by Clayton Christensen, James Allworth, and Karen Dillon—a book that takes the management theories Christensen taught at Harvard Business School and applies them to one of the most important questions we can ask ourselves: What makes for a fulfilling life?Rather than relying on inspirational advice, Christensen argues that the same principles that help companies succeed can also help us make better decisions about our careers, relationships, and personal lives.I spend most of the episode focusing on three ideas that stood out to me.First is the concept of deliberate strategy vs. emergent strategy. We often believe we need to map out every step of our future, but Christensen shows that many of the most successful companies—and people—arrive somewhere they never originally planned. The key isn't predicting the future; it's having clear values while remaining flexible enough to recognize and pursue unexpected opportunities.Next, I dive into motivation theory, exploring why money and external rewards only go so far. Christensen draws on research showing that while fair compensation matters, long-term fulfillment comes from meaningful work, opportunities to grow, responsibility, and making a genuine contribution. It's a useful framework for thinking about both our own careers and how we lead teams.Finally, we discuss outsourcing—not just as a business decision, but as a life decision. Which responsibilities should you keep because they create meaning or strengthen relationships, and which are better delegated so you can spend more time on what matters most?
