The 4 C’s Formula:

Commitment → Courage → Capability → Confidence

In this episode, I explore Dan Sullivan’s 4 C’s Formula and why confidence doesn’t come before taking action—it comes after.

When you commit to a goal that’s bigger than anything you’ve done before, you’re almost guaranteed to face uncertainty. Moving forward anyway requires courage. That courage forces you to develop new capabilities, and those capabilities ultimately create confidence.

I talk about how this framework applies to entrepreneurship, personal growth, and doing things before you feel ready—and why waiting until you feel confident may be exactly what keeps you stuck.

Commitment → Courage → Capability → Confidence.
