Episode 10

The Millionaire Master Plan: Your Personalized Path to Financial Success by Roger James Hamilton

August 9, 2026

What if the path to becoming a millionaire isn’t the same for everyone?

In this episode of Taylor Made Thoughts, I explore Roger James Hamilton’s The Millionaire Master Plan and its central idea: building wealth isn’t just about working harder or following someone else’s formula—it’s about understanding where you are now and choosing the right strategy for your particular stage.

I break down Hamilton’s framework for moving through different levels of wealth, why the skills that get you to one level may not get you to the next, and how your approach to making money should evolve as your resources, experience, and opportunities change.

Along the way, I look at what I found useful in the book, where I’m skeptical, and how its ideas apply to entrepreneurs trying to figure out what they should actually be focusing on next.

If you’ve ever felt like you’re doing a lot but aren’t sure whether you’re working on the right things to build wealth, this framework offers an interesting way to think about the problem.
